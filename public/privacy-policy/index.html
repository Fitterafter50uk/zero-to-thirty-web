Privacy Policy

Zero to Thirty

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Introduction

Zero to Thirty is a running application designed to help users develop their running ability, track sessions and monitor progress towards running 30 minutes continuously.

Information We Collect

The app may use your device's location while you record a run to measure distance, track your route and calculate running statistics. If you enable background location, location tracking may continue while the app is in the background during an active session.

The app may store running sessions, programme progress, personal bests and preferences on your device.

Community and Leaderboard Features

If you use community or leaderboard features, information you choose to submit, such as a display name, running results or community posts, may be transmitted to our Supabase-hosted backend to provide those features. Do not submit information you do not want other users to see. Community and leaderboard information may be visible to other users of the app.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide running-session tracking, route and distance measurements, programme progress, personal records, leaderboards and community features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising. Information submitted to online community or leaderboard features is processed by the services used to operate those features.

Data Storage and Security

Some information is stored locally on your device. Information submitted through online features may be stored and processed by Supabase. We take reasonable steps to protect information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Location Permissions

Location permission is used for running-related features that need your position. Background location may be used to continue recording an active run when the app is not in the foreground. You can manage or revoke location permissions through your device settings. Revoking permission may affect route and distance tracking.

Data Deletion

You can remove locally stored app information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it, although this may not delete information already submitted to online services.

To request deletion of personal information associated with online features, email craig.potterhill@outlook.com. Please provide enough information to identify the relevant data. We will review your request and delete information where we are able to do so, subject to legal obligations and any applicable service requirements.

Children's Privacy

Zero to Thirty is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information where consent is required by law.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or wish to request data deletion, contact:

craig.potterhill@outlook.com
